About Green Leaf

Green Leaf began in 2016 as a small family nursery with one greenhouse and a simple idea: make it easy for anyone to grow plants at home. Today we grow and source over 120 varieties of indoor and outdoor plants, all raised locally.

On every visit, our team shares honest advice on light, watering and soil - so your plants keep growing long after you take them home.
